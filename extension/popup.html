About Dark Patterns

Dark patterns are deceptive design techniques that trick users into making decisions they wouldn't otherwise make. This extension helps identify and alert you about such patterns.

Covered Laws:

  • GDPR - EU Privacy Law
  • CCPA/CPRA - California Privacy
  • FTC Act §5 - Deceptive Practices
  • ROSCA - Subscription Rules
